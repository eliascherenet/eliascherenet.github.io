About Me

I am PhD student at University of New England Australia, School of ERS, originally from Haramaya University, Ethiopia. I hold a BSc in Natural Resources from Bahir Dar University, Ethiopia, and an MSc in Geoinformation Science and Earth Observation from the University of Twente (ITC), the Netherlands. My research focuses on drought monitoring and assessment using Earth Observation data and machine learning techniques across Eastern Africa, where I integrate satellite, climate and geospatial data to develop integrated drought models that support ecosystem resilience, sustainable land management, and climate adaptation. I am passionate about applying geospatial technologies and data-driven approaches to strengthen early warning systems,climate-resilient livestock systems and inform environmental decision-making in dryland and semi-arid pastoralist regions, where drought is the major climate change-related problem.
